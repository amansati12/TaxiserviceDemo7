Who We Are

Crafting Extraordinary Journeys

LuxRide was founded on a simple belief: transportation should be an experience, not merely a utility. Since 2016, we've redefined what it means to travel in India — merging world-class luxury standards with local excellence.

Every vehicle in our fleet is meticulously maintained. Every chauffeur is handpicked and trained. Every journey is a reflection of our commitment to impeccable service.

Our Journey

A Legacy of Excellence

Every milestone tells the story of our relentless pursuit of luxury and service quality.

2016
The Beginning
LuxRide launches in Ahmedabad with 5 premium vehicles. First corporate client: a Fortune 500 company's India office.
2017
Airport Division
Dedicated airport transfer service launches with real-time flight tracking and professional meet & greet services.
2019
National Expansion
Expansion to Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore. Fleet grows to 100+ vehicles. Awarded Best Premium Taxi by Travel India Magazine.
2021
Electric Fleet Initiative
Tesla Model 3 and Model S join the fleet, pioneering eco-luxury transport. Carbon offset programme launched.
2024
15,000+ Rides
LuxRide celebrates 15,000+ completed rides, 200+ chauffeurs, 25 cities, and a 4.9-star average rating. The journey continues.
