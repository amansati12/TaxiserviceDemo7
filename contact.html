Common Questions

How quickly can I get confirmed?
Instant bookings receive confirmation within 60 seconds via SMS and email. For same-day bookings, we dispatch a driver within 15–30 minutes.
Do you operate outside Gujarat?
Yes — 25+ cities across India including Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Chennai. Long-distance intercity transfers available on request.
How do I report an issue?
Call +91 98765 99999 immediately if you're in the vehicle. For post-ride complaints, email complaint@luxride.in — we guarantee response within 2 hours.
Can I book for someone else?
Absolutely. Enter the passenger's name and contact number during booking — they receive confirmation and driver details directly.
