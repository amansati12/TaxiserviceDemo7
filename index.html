Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ride?
Book via our website, mobile app, or call 24/7. Enter pickup, destination, date and time, choose your vehicle, confirm — done. Instant SMS and email confirmation follows immediately.
How far in advance should I book?
2–4 hours for city rides, 24 hours for airport transfers. For weddings and special events, book several weeks ahead to secure your preferred vehicle class and route planning.
Are all your drivers background-verified?
Every LuxRide chauffeur undergoes comprehensive background checks, holds a valid commercial licence, and completes our in-house training in safety, etiquette, and luxury hospitality standards.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 2 hours before pickup. Cancellations within 2 hours incur a 50% charge. No-shows are charged in full. Contact us for emergencies — we always try to accommodate.
Do you offer corporate accounts?
Yes — priority booking, monthly billing with GST invoices, dedicated account manager, and custom reporting. Contact our business team for a tailored package that fits your organisation.
